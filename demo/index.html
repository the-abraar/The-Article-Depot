The Article Depot

An unattended pipeline meant to read Google Trends, write explainers grounded in the news behind each trend, veto anything it can't support, and prune pages when the topic goes cold.

Status: 0 articles published Built and tested offline (69 tests, no network). Never launched: the daily workflow has been disabled since 21 Aug 2026 and has 0 runs.

Pipeline

Trends RSS→polite fetch
robots.txt, 2 s delay, backoff→extract sources
≥ 2 or skip→writer call
never run→3-layer veto
audit never run→publish / prune
0 pages

Solid boxes have run against real sites. Dashed boxes have only run in tests, against scripted model replies.

The veto: any layer rejects

  1. Structure (free). Rejects: under 400 or over 1,600 words; fewer than 2 sections; empty title or over 120 characters; no recorded claims; placeholder text; a claim citing a source that was never fetched; a section with no paragraphs.
  2. Duplicate (free). Rejects: word-set similarity 0.6 or more against any published article.
  3. Grounding audit (a second model call). Rejects: grounding or clarity below 4 of 5; any verdict other than "publish"; even one claim the sources don't support.

Source: pipeline/quality.py, thresholds in pipeline/config.py. Each rejection is stored with its reason.
